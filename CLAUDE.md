# OmniA2S

Reverse engineering of the Hotone Ampero II Stomp firmware (V2.5.0). Read `docs/HANDOFF.md` first, then `docs/firmware-analysis.md` and `docs/tooling.md`.
Never commit firmware, vendor software or manuals (`firmware/` is gitignored). Always keep a stock-firmware backup; the pedal has a force-update recovery mode.
